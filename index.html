M.Sc. thesis · Bauhaus-Universität Weimar

Clone Testing

An investigation of trust and suspicion in identity recognition during human-machine dialogue

Shriya Singh Supervised by Prof. Dr. Benno Stein and Prof. Dr. Maria Wolters Prototype recording, 4 min 48 s

This is the study system from the participant's side. An evaluator holds a live text conversation with a partner who is either a real person or an LLM-based clone trained by that same person, and rates how suspicious they are after every single turn rather than once at the end.

What happens in the recording

Why the per-turn slider is the point. Across the study, evaluators identified 19 of 20 conversations correctly. Underneath that number, trust frequently reversed mid-conversation, including evaluators recovering confidence in a partner they had already begun to doubt, and the clearest cues were stylistic rather than factual.
